# Miyar 6.0: what changed since you last tried it

**To:** the Miyar team
**From:** Prof. Adeeb Noor
**Date:** 29 September 2026

Several of you tried earlier versions and hit the same problems. Some screens looked empty or unfinished. Results did not connect from one step to the next. It was hard to tell what the numbers meant. Before 6.0, an HR expert went through every screen, and the technical review team's 18 requirements were worked through one by one. This is what came out of that.

## What you will notice in the first minute

- **A "What's new" panel** on the first visit. Each item opens the screen it describes.
- **A guided 10-minute tour.** Press *Start the guided tour*. It loads clearly labelled demo data and walks you through five screens, so you see filled-in, connected results instead of blank forms.
- **"New" badges** in the menu on the sections that changed. They disappear once you visit.
- **Plain-English conclusions.** Planning and compensation results now start with *What this means for your decision*.

## What actually works differently

| You told us | What changed |
|---|---|
| The grade from job evaluation did not match the grade structure, and did not reach compensation | Points now map to your approved grade (for example 750 points → G13), and the grade carries into Compensation automatically. |
| Manpower results said "Hire 1" and "Build 1" as if they were two people | The gap is split into backfill (already budgeted) and growth (needs budget approval). Five alternatives follow — Buy, Build, Borrow, Bind, Bot — with the effect of each lever on the gap. |
| Compensation only showed a number | It now checks the salary band itself, explains the compa-ratio, shows the cost of bringing pay up to the minimum, and breaks down the package cost. |
| Some text was invisible or hard to read, and English terms appeared in the Arabic screens | Contrast is fixed to accessibility standards, labels are translated, and pages open at the top. |
| We were not sure it was secure enough for real accounts | Two-step sign-in (an authenticator-app code) is available for organization accounts. Administrators can reset it, and each reset is logged. |
| Demos depended on luck | There is a permanent demo organization with synthetic data, plus one-click demo data in the browser. |

## What has not changed yet — honestly

- **Occupation reference.** The occupation list is still the supplied 2019 edition until the newer official edition is licensed.
- **AI engine.** The strategic AI engine runs on a trial window. Its extension and paid API access are being arranged.
- **Sign-in.** Single sign-on and e-mail password recovery are not available yet. An administrator still issues recovery links.
- **Real employee data.** Do not enter real employee data until the data-processing agreement is signed.

## What we ask from you

1. Open https://adeebnoor.github.io/Miyar/?v=6.0.1#home and take the 10-minute guided tour.
2. Try one real (non-confidential) case from your own work.
3. Send one thing that still feels wrong, with a screenshot if you can.

Thank you for your patience with the earlier versions. Your feedback shaped this release.
