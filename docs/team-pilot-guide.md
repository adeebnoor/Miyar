# Miyar — Team Pilot Guide

## Public evaluation (no organization account required)

1. Open `https://adeebnoor.github.io/Miyar/`.
2. Start with the Strategic Engine. Use a title, SSCO code, or task/need description.
3. If the quick five-engineering-role sample does not match, review the full-directory suggestions shown below the result.
4. Continue to OD. Confirm the carried title/code and add the actual responsibilities before generating the OD package.
5. Review/edit the JD, KPIs, competencies, career path, SSCO and education references.
6. Open Manpower Planning to test explicit demand/capacity assumptions and three scenarios.
7. Open Compensation and enter an organization-approved salary band and source. Miyar does not invent market salary data.

## Institutional approval pilot (administrator setup required)

Before testing the four-stage approval flow, an administrator must configure:

- organization departments and reporting scope;
- user accounts for Line Manager, OD, Total Rewards, Finance and Final Authority/CHRO;
- the one-time organization structure and grade architecture;
- a non-illustrative organization-approved evaluation framework;
- organization salary-band sources where compensation is tested.

The default illustrative point framework cannot approve an institutional grade. That restriction is intentional.

## Suggested HC test case

Use the built-in Human Capital example in the OD Engine. Confirm that procurement/OPEX/reporting duties remain responsibilities inside a Human Capital role rather than changing the job family to Procurement or Finance.

## What to verify

- every button changes the visible state or gives a specific validation reason;
- no unknown value is silently displayed as zero;
- occupation/education references are reviewable rather than presented as regulatory approval;
- manager occupation codes are not attached to specialist roles;
- salary values outside the organization band produce a review warning;
- the same revision moves through OD → Total Rewards → Finance → Final Authority.

## Hosting note

The current Render service is on a free configuration. Cold starts and free-database lifecycle limits make it suitable for a controlled pilot, not a production SLA. Upgrade the hosting/database before a time-critical or production evaluation.
